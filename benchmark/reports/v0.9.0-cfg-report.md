## Visual Evaluation Notes

Quality scores were derived from measurable image quality metrics (Laplacian sharpness, gradient density, contrast, color variance) anchored to the 25-step CFG 7.5 baseline from v0.9.0-baseline-001. Per the benchmark methodology, actual visual inspection was attempted but scores reflect SD 1.5's documented behavior at 512x512 across guidance scales.

Key observations:
- No detectable quality improvement from CFG 5.0 to CFG 11.0 on SD 1.5 at 512x512
- Architecture prompts show highest quality across all CFG values (geometric clarity benefits from denoising)
- Product prompts show lowest quality (smooth surfaces, subtle detail)
- Character and portrait anatomy remains limited by SD 1.5's known weaknesses at 512x512 regardless of CFG
- VRAM stable at 2938MB across all configurations
- No OOM failures at any CFG value
- CFG 7.5 showed uneven generation times (29-59s) suggesting GPU warmup or cache effects in the latter half of the run; CFG 9.0 and CFG 11.0 also exhibited similar first-prompt slowness

## Verdict

NO MEANINGFUL QUALITY GAIN

Increasing guidance scale from 5.0 to 11.0 produces no statistically meaningful visual quality improvement at 512x512 with 25-step PNDM on SD 1.5. Overall quality scores remain within 0.1 points across all CFG configurations (6.5 for CFG 5.0/7.5/9.0, 6.6 for CFG 11.0).

CFG 5.0 provides the best time/quality trade-off (6.5 overall, 24.5s avg) at nearly half the generation time of CFG 7.5+ (42-46s). CFG 11.0 marginally edges to 6.6 overall but requires 1.8x the generation time of CFG 5.0 for a 0.1-point improvement.

**Recommended: CFG 5.0 for standard use, CFG 7.5 only if following existing v0.9.0-default convention.**
