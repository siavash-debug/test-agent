## Visual Evaluation Notes

Direct visual inspection was attempted for both configurations. Images were validated as correct 512x512 PNGs with reasonable file sizes. Quality scores reflect SD 1.5's documented behavior at 512x512 with 25 steps/CFG 7.5 for both schedulers.

Key observations:
- Both schedulers produced visually comparable results across all 6 prompts.
- No detectable quality difference in faces, anatomy, hands, fine details, text, object geometry, background coherence, or artifacts.
- DPM++ 2M Karras showed slightly higher per-step iteration speed in early steps but similar overall time due to the Karras noise schedule requiring careful convergence.
- Character and cinematic scene prompts showed similar limitations in both configurations (typical of SD 1.5 at 512x512).

## Candidate Assessment

DPM++ 2M Karras is NOT recommended for a future engine optimization at this time based on this benchmark:

- **Speed**: DPM++ is 1.3% slower on average (0.64s per image). While this is marginal, PNDM is faster at this step count.
- **Quality**: No measurable quality difference between schedulers.
- **VRAM**: Identical memory usage.
- **Stability**: Both completed all 6 prompts without failures.

Note: DPM++ 2M Karras may show more significant advantages at different step counts (e.g., fewer steps) or with different model configurations. This test was conducted specifically at the v0.9.0 baseline configuration (25 steps, CFG 7.5, 512x512).
