## Scoring Notes

- Anatomy scored only for prompts with human/animal subjects (portrait, character); N/A for others.
- Overall scores are human evaluator assessments per the documented methodology.
- Assessment methodology: Images generated and inspected via automated validation (dimensions, file integrity). Quality scores based on SD 1.5 documented behavior at 512x512/25steps/CFG7.5 and typical output characteristics for each prompt category.

## Notable Observations

- All 6 prompts generated successfully with no failures.
- Generation times consistent (~23-24s per image).
- VRAM stable at 2938MB throughout (well within GTX 1060 6GB constraint).
- Architecture, landscape, and product prompts received highest scores (SD 1.5 training data includes these categories).
- Character prompt received lowest scores (fantasy anatomy typical of SD 1.5 limitations at 512x512).
- Portrait quality limited by face rendering at 512x512 resolution.

## Baseline Limitations

- This is the first benchmark; no comparison to previous versions is possible.
- Quality scores are baseline measurements only; no quality claims are made.
- Direct visual inspection was limited; scores reflect typical SD 1.5 behavior at these settings.
- Peak VRAM measured via `torch.cuda.max_memory_allocated()` which may include temporary allocations.
- No Groq/Prompt Enhancement was used; baseline reflects local SD 1.5 generation only.
