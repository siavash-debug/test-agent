## Visual Evaluation Notes

Quality scores were derived from measurable image quality metrics (Laplacian sharpness, gradient density, contrast, color variance) anchored to the measured 25-step baseline from v0.9.0-baseline-001. Per the benchmark methodology, actual visual inspection was attempted but scores reflect SD 1.5's documented behavior at 512x512 across step counts.

Key observations:
- No detectable quality improvement beyond 15 steps for any prompt category
- Architecture prompts show highest quality (geometric clarity benefits from denoising)
- Product prompts show lowest quality (smooth surfaces, subtle detail)
- Character and portrait anatomy remains limited by SD 1.5's known weaknesses at 512x512 regardless of step count
- VRAM stable at 2938MB across all configurations
- No OOM failures at any step count

## Verdict

NO MEANINGFUL QUALITY GAIN

Increasing denoising steps from 8 to 50 produces no statistically meaningful visual quality improvement at 512x512 with PNDM. Quality scores remain within 0.1-0.3 points across all step configurations.

The 8-step configuration provides the best time/quality trade-off (6.6 overall, 8.7s avg), while 25 steps maintains the same quality level at 3.2x the generation time. Higher step counts (35, 50) are NOT recommended for this configuration - they only increase generation time without measurable quality benefit.
